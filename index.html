"I like fixing things that are technically 'working' but obviously broken — a thousand paper trainee assessment forms sitting in a filing system being one of them."

That was my starting point as an AI/ML Intern at Tata Motors. Instead of manually reading and scoring each record, I built a pipeline that reads the scans itself: OpenCV and Tesseract at the front end doing the heavy lifting on messy, inconsistent paper quality, a custom preprocessing layer to squeeze out noise, and Scikit-learn models underneath to make sense of the data once clean.

What used to take days now takes hours — evaluation time dropped by roughly 80%, and 10+ stakeholders went from waiting on a weekly Excel file to watching a live dashboard update in real time.

That project pulled me deeper into two focus areas: Computer Vision and Generative AI (RAG).
